Frequently Asked Questions

Are your diamonds ethically sourced?

Yes. We strictly adhere to the Kimberley Process and only source diamonds from ethical, conflict-free mines. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward unethically mined stones.

Do you offer bespoke or custom designs?

Absolutely. Our master artisans are available for private commissions. Please book a Virtual Consultation or visit one of our flagship boutiques to begin the process.

What is your shipping policy?

We offer complimentary, fully insured international shipping on all high-jewellery pieces. Deliveries are made via secure courier and require an adult signature.
